# Credits

CustomSlop ships **code only**. It never contains, copies into this repository, or
redistributes any game model, texture, sound or font. At run time it reads the content you
already have installed (your TF2 Classified server, TF2, and any optional donor folders you
configure) and ships its own *mutated derivatives* of donor weapons to your own FastDL. If you
publish those derivatives, keep the donor credits below with them.

## Donor weapon sources (read from your installs, never bundled)

| Source | Credit |
|---|---|
| TF2 Classified stock weapons | TF2 Classified team |
| TF2 Classified April Fools 2026 pack | TF2 Classified team (official 3.0.11 release) |
| Open Fortress Mercenary weapons (via a tf2cc staging tree) | Open Fortress team |
| MarxVee custom-classes pack (via a tf2cc staging tree) | MarxVee pack authors |
| 4dm / 4rdm "presplit" weapons (map paks) | TF2C Presplit / TDC team |
| Old TF2 Classic 2.0.0 beta weapons | TF2 Classic team |
| Team Fortress 2 / Half-Life 2 models and sounds referenced in place | Valve |

Each generated weapon records its donor and the matching credit line in its `summary.json`.

## Fonts

The item cards use TF2's "TF2 Build" and "TF2 Secondary" fonts, loaded from your own TF2
install at run time. They are not included here.
